Every month, millions of people struggle with complex forms. Government applications, insurance claims, tax documents. Small business owners spend forty-five minutes or more per form, reading fine print, googling terms, and calling helplines. One wrong field can delay processing by weeks.

Meet FormPilot. Upload a screenshot of any form, describe your situation in plain English, and get field-by-field fill instructions with suggested values.

FormPilot uses Gemini Vision to analyze form screenshots. It detects every visible field, understands what each one means, and generates specific fill instructions based on your context.

Let me show you. I upload a screenshot of a tax form and type: I'm a sole trader, earned seventy-five thousand dollars, single, no dependents.

Gemini Vision analyzes the screenshot and returns a structured analysis. Each field is identified with its name, type, suggested value, fill instructions, and any warnings about common mistakes.

The numbered markers overlay on the form image show exactly where each field is located. This makes it immediately clear which instruction corresponds to which field.

The step-by-step checklist mode lets you check off fields as you fill them, with completion tracking. No more second-guessing which fields you've already handled.

FormPilot also keeps a history of all your analyses. You can browse previous forms with thumbnails and revisit any analysis.

Here's the architecture. The Next.js frontend handles drag-and-drop image upload and sends the screenshot plus user context to a FastAPI backend on Cloud Run. The backend sends both to Gemini 2.5 Flash Vision API, which returns structured JSON with field names, types, suggested values, instructions, warnings, and approximate positions.

The backend runs on Google Cloud Run with auto-scaling. API keys are stored in Secret Manager. SQLite handles persistence. A single deploy script automates the entire infrastructure setup.

FormPilot transforms confusing forms from a forty-five minute ordeal into a two-minute guided process. With Gemini Vision's form understanding, anyone can fill out any form correctly the first time.
